Mow Tycoon

Mow. Sell. Upgrade. Prestige.

Mow grass, sell it, upgrade your tools and prestige through 14 worlds.

Free on AndroidComing soon to Google Play

Grab your rusty shears and start mowing. Fill your backpack, sell the grass, buy better tools and turn a small lawn into a mowing empire.

What’s inside

Mow with one thumb

Drag anywhere to walk. Your tool cuts everything in reach on its own, so you can play with one hand.

Eight tools to master

Work your way up from Rusty Shears and a Scythe to a Riding Mower, a Combine Harvester, a Laser Cutter and the Crystal Scythe. Upgrade power, range and speed, then reforge your tool for a random trait.

Fourteen worlds

Desert, swamp, jungle, snow, beach, a crystal cavern and more. Every world has its own look, its own music and more valuable grass. Find keys in the grass to open doors and treasure chests.

Prestige and grow

Reset your run to earn permanent bonuses and talent points. Each prestige unlocks something new: a robot factory, a skill tree, daily quests, pets, and a new system every five levels.

Robots that work while you are away

Build a factory and send mowing robots to any world you have opened. They keep mowing for up to eight hours while the game is closed.

Pets, hats and trails

Hatch pets from chests, each with its own bonus. Dress your character with hats, outfits, tool skins and trails.

Long goals

48 achievements pay gems, a daily reward waits every day, and after Prestige 50 you can replant the lawn for golden seeds that make every sale worth more.

Season events

Every four weeks the event world changes its theme. Collect season tokens and trade them for exclusive rewards. A free Season Pass has 30 tiers of rewards and a season-only robot skin.

Questions

Is the game free?

Yes. You can play the whole game for free. Ads appear only when you choose to watch one for a reward, and purchases are optional.

Do I need an internet connection?

No, the game plays offline. The internet is only used for the ads you choose to watch and for purchases.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your phone. To keep it safe, open Menu > Backup, tap COPY MY SAVE and keep the code somewhere safe. After reinstalling, paste it in the same place and tap LOAD THIS CODE.

I bought something but did not get it.

Open the Shop and tap Restore purchases. If that does not help, write to us with the date of the purchase.

How do I change what ads may use?

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks before showing any ad. You can change your answer at any time under Menu > Ad privacy choices.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. The game is coming to Android first.

Support

Questions, a lost save or an idea for the game? Write to support@virenzo.com. In the game, Menu > Contact support starts an e-mail with your game version already filled in.
