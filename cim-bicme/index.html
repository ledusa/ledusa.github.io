About the game

Grab your rusty shears and start mowing. Fill your backpack, sell the grass, buy better tools and turn a small lawn into a mowing empire. Drag anywhere to walk: your tool cuts everything in reach on its own, so you can play with one hand.

  • Eight tools, from Rusty Shears to the Crystal Scythe
  • Fourteen worlds, each with its own look and music
  • Prestige for permanent bonuses, a skill tree and a robot factory that mows while you are away
  • Pets, hats, outfits and trails; season events and a free Season Pass
  • Plays offline. Ads only when you choose to watch one; purchases are optional
  • English, Turkish, Spanish and Portuguese

Support

Questions, a lost save or an idea for the game: support@virenzo.com. Your progress is stored only on your phone; Menu > Backup copies it as a save code you can keep.
